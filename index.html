Hand gestures

Turn on hand control with the hand button, then allow the front camera. Hold each gesture still for a moment.

  • Thumb up or point up — start play
  • Open palm or thumb down — stop
  • Hand Up, Hand Down, Pinky Down, Ring Down, Middle Down, or Palm Down — pause and hold the 3D hand on that pose until another gesture or Play
