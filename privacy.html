Overview

Talam Putter (“the App”) is published by Flipstereo. This policy describes how the App handles information.

Information we collect

The App does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as name, email, or location for our own analytics.

  • Purchases: Unlock purchases are processed by Apple through In-App Purchase. We do not receive your full payment card details. Apple may process purchase-related data under Apple’s privacy policy.
  • On-device data: The App may store preferences and unlock status locally on your device (for example, so a purchase remains available after restart). This data stays on your device unless you delete the App.

Tracking and advertising

The App does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising.

Children

The App is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
