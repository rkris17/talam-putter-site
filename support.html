Hand gestures

Hand control uses the front camera. Tap the hand button, tap OK, and allow the camera. Hold each gesture still for a moment so it can be read. Tap the hand button again to turn it off.

  • Thumb up or point up — start play
  • Open palm or thumb down — stop
  • Hand Up, Hand Down, Pinky Down, Ring Down, Middle Down, or Palm Down — pause playback and hold the 3D hand on that pose until you make another gesture or tap Play

What is free vs unlock

  • Free: Adi Talam playback with free-tier limits (for example cycle and tempo limits).
  • Unlock (one-time purchase): all talam clips and unlimited playback.

Restore a purchase

If you already bought the unlock and the app looks locked again (new phone, reinstall), open the app, select a premium talam if needed, and tap Restore. Use the same Apple ID that made the purchase.
